# Privacy Policy — DJ

Last updated: 12 November 2026

DJ is a private, local-only laptop assistant. It finds and opens your
files by name or by meaning, organizes folders, and finds duplicate
files. It is built for one person: you, on your own computer.

## What DJ does NOT do

- **No account.** DJ has no sign-up, no login, no profile.
- **No cloud.** DJ has no server. Nothing you search, open, organize,
  or delete is sent to us or to anyone else.
- **No tracking.** DJ collects no analytics, no usage statistics,
  no crash reports, and no advertising identifiers.
- **No ads.** DJ shows no ads and contains no ad software.
- **No microphone or camera.** DJ never records audio or video.
- **No internet needed.** DJ works fully offline. The small
  meaning-search model is inside the app itself.

## What stays on your computer

Everything DJ knows lives only on your own computer, in the app's
private data folder (`%APPDATA%\DJ` on Windows):

- The list of folders you asked DJ to look in.
- The search index DJ builds from those folders (file names, paths,
  and the text inside your documents, so DJ can find them by meaning).
- Your settings.

DJ never indexes your whole drive — only the folders you choose.
You can remove a folder at any time, and you can delete DJ's data
folder to erase everything DJ stored.

## Updates

- **Microsoft Store version:** updates come through the Microsoft
  Store. DJ does not check for updates itself.
- **Version installed from GitHub:** DJ checks GitHub Releases for a
  newer version, downloads it in the background, and installs it
  when you restart DJ. That check sends no personal information —
  only a request for the latest release information.

## Files you open

When you ask DJ to open a file or folder, DJ opens it with the
program already on your computer (for example, your document app or
File Explorer). DJ does not upload, copy, or share the file.
